Magic WandmagicWand
 matches
When your hero attacks in battle, there is a 25% chance that the struck enemy stack turns into creatures of the attacker's type. The total health of the stack is preserved.

Only living enemies are affected; the converted stack stays on the enemy side.
  • TRANSMUTATION 25%
  • Subtype transmutationPerHealth — amount based on preserved health
  • addInfo not set → transforms into the attacker
  • propagator: ARMY
  • bonuses#override — replaces all previous bonuses
 Randomly replaced the hero's equipped artifacts every day. The pair of slots depended on which Misc slot the wand occupied.Original: weekly artifact transformation, impossible to reproduce in VCMI. Replaced with combat transmutation of enemies. Magic Wand
Heroes III has no such artifact — it was added by the Wake of Gods expansion. Originally it randomly replaced the hero's equipped artifacts every day.
